## ☕ Why this exists

At home we have a moka pot, a milk frother and a family that loves a good cup. We kept wondering why the coffee came out bitter one day and watery the next.

Café recipes assume café machines. Ours is a stovetop pot that makes 300 ml, a frother that runs on batteries, and a jug that holds 350 ml. So we built the guide we wished we had:

- **It does the maths.** Tell it how many people. It works out the water, the coffee, the milk and how many times to brew, for *your* pot and *your* jug.
- **It stays with you.** One big, clear step per screen, with a timer when you need one. No scrolling through a wall of text with wet hands.
- **It helps you get better.** Everyone rates their own cup. If something was off, it tells you exactly what to change next time.
- **It speaks your language.** Flip any step to **Banglish** with one tap. My mom knows what a latte is. She just reads the steps better in Bangla.

No account. No ads. No server. Your progress lives on your phone.

## ✨ Features

### 1. Your kitchen, set up in 30 seconds

<img src="docs/screens/01-setup.webp" alt="Equipment setup" width="270" align="right">

The first time you open the app it asks three quick questions: **Let's get your equipment ready.**

- **Moka pot size:** 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 9 or 12 cup. This sets how much water and coffee go in each brew.
- **Grinder:** electric, manual or none. The grinding step changes to match: short pulses, a number of turns, or ready ground coffee.
- **Milk pitcher:** 350, 500, 600 or 1000 ml. This sets the fill marks for frothing and how many batches you need.

Change any of it later in **Pantry** and every recipe updates straight away.

<br clear="right">

### 2. Fourteen drinks, with names you already know

**Hot:** Espresso · Italian Moka · Americano · Cappuccino · Latte · Flat White · Mocha · Café au Lait

**Cold:** Iced Latte · Iced Mocha · Whipped Coffee

**Frappes:** Butterscotch · Dark Chocolate · Cookies & Creme

**Italian Moka** is made the way Italian homes have done it since 1933: cold water, low heat, off at the first gurgle, and a *cremina* of sugar whipped with the first drops of coffee. **Frappes** chill the coffee first, then blend it with milk and ice until thick and frosty.

Pick a drink, then choose:

- **People:** from 1 up. The app works out how many brews that takes.
- **Strength:** Mild, Normal or Strong. It always stays inside the drink's proper café ratio, so a Strong Latte is still a latte.
- **Bean roast:** Light to Dark. The grind and timing advice adjusts.
- **Sugar:** None to Extra.

Each drink card shows **Ready to make** when your Pantry has everything, and your best rating so far.

### 3. Step by step, in English or Banglish

<p align="center">
  <img src="docs/screens/strip-en-bn.webp" alt="The same step in English and in Banglish" width="560">
</p>
<p align="center"><sub>One tap on <b>EN | BN</b>: the same step, in English or Banglish</sub></p>

This is the heart of the app. Instead of one long recipe, you get **one step per screen**:

- A **progress bar** so you always know how far along you are.
- Big **Back** and **Next** buttons, or just swipe.
- **Built-in timers** for the parts that need watching, like the moka pot brew.
- **The screen stays awake** while you cook. No unlocking with coffee on your fingers.
- **Leave and come back.** Exit keeps your place: the drink page offers **Resume at step 4** (or start over).
- **A moving picture for every action.** See the section below.
- **EN | BN switch** at the top. Steps and advice switch to Banglish. Drink names stay in English on purpose.

The order follows how a real barista works: kettle on first, grind while the water heats, brew, cool the base, then heat and froth the milk right before serving so nothing sits around going cold.

### 4. Every step comes alive

<picture>
  <source media="(prefers-color-scheme: dark)" srcset="docs/scenes-dark.webp">
  <img src="docs/scenes-light.webp" alt="Twelve of the step scenes: kettle, grinder, filling the moka base, levelling the basket, brewing, cooling, heating milk, frothing, cremina, ice bath, blending and latte art" width="100%">
</picture>

Every step is a small animated scene on **the same kitchen counter**, so moving through a recipe feels like staying in one kitchen. Nothing floats: the pot sits on the burner, the tap comes out of the wall, the kettle pours in from the edge of the frame.

- The **kettle** heats while a thermometer climbs. The **grinder** buzzes with beans tumbling inside, and a pile of grounds grows beside it. A **manual grinder's crank** turns.
- Water **rises to the valve line** and a tick appears when it gets there. Grounds fall into the basket and get **levelled**.
- The burner **knob turns to your heat setting** and the flames light. The **moka pot** shows coffee rising inside its top chamber, with your cup waiting next to it. On **low heat** the flames are smaller.
- The **cold tap** hits the base while a thermometer drops. **Milk** steams in the pan with the thermometer heading for 65°C. The **frother** spins as foam climbs to the mark on your pitcher.
- The **cremina** turns from dark to golden as you whisk. Frappes get an **ice bath** and a **blender vortex**. Iced drinks get ice cubes dropping into each glass, one glass per person.

**When a timer runs, the scene follows it.** Start the 5 minute brew and the coffee in the pot rises with the clock. Start the froth and the foam climbs as the seconds go. Start the ice bath and the thermometer falls. You can glance at the picture from across the kitchen and know how far along it is.

<p align="center">
  <img src="docs/screens/strip-alive.webp" alt="A frappe blending with its timer running, and the finish screen with XP" width="560">
</p>

When you finish, **your drink is poured and fills up layer by layer**, in that recipe's real proportions. Milk drinks get **latte art on the foam**, black coffee gets crema, iced drinks get ice and a straw, frappes get the whipped top. Then confetti, and an XP card **counts up** with exactly what you earned (+20 made it, +30 first time, +25 Today's Special), plus any level-up or new badge.

The pictures follow the step, never the language, so they're the same in English and Banglish. If your phone is set to **Reduce motion**, nothing moves, but the scenes still show where you are: the coffee level still follows the timer.

### 5. Everyone at the table rates their own cup

<img src="docs/screens/06-rate-person.webp" alt="Rating per person" width="270" align="right">

Made coffee for three? Pass the phone around. **Person 1, Person 2, Person 3**, each gives their own stars and can say what was off:

- Too bitter
- Sour or thin
- Too weak
- Burnt taste
- Foam was poor
- Too sweet, or not sweet enough

The app then shows **a real fix for each problem**, written for your exact grinder and roast. For example: *"Take it off the heat earlier. Try shorter grinder pulses for a slightly coarser grind."*

Tap 5 stars and they pop, with a little burst of confetti. Forgot to rate? No problem. Drinks you skipped wait for you in **Learn**, and you can rate them later.

### 6. Learn, earn XP and level up

<p align="center">
  <img src="docs/screens/strip-learn.webp" alt="Your level and next move, how XP works, a quiz, and a level-up" width="100%">
</p>
<p align="center"><sub><b>Your level and next move</b> &nbsp;·&nbsp; <b>How XP works</b> &nbsp;·&nbsp; <b>Quick quizzes</b> &nbsp;·&nbsp; <b>Level up</b></sub></p>

Getting better at coffee should feel like a game, not homework.

- **5 topics:** The Drinks, Moka Pot, Beans & Grinding, Milk & Foam, and Safety. Short lessons, then a 3 question quiz.
- **5 levels:** Beginner, Apprentice, Home Barista, Skilled Barista, Coffee Master.
- **11 badges** and a **day streak** for coming back. A flame on the drink list shows your streak, and warns you when it's about to end.
- **Today's Special:** one drink a day, picked from what you can make right now, worth **+25 XP**. Make three for the **Chef's Pick** badge.
- **Level-ups and new badges get their own moment**: a card with confetti, wherever you earn them.
- **"Your next move"** always tells you the single best thing to do to level up.
- **Nothing ever goes backwards.** Badges stay once earned, and XP from old drinks is kept, however long you use the app.

**How you earn XP**

| Do this | Get |
|---|---|
| Make a drink, start to finish | **+20** |
| Make a drink you've never made before | **+30** more |
| Rate it | **+10** |
| Each extra person who rates | **+5** |
| Anyone gives it 5 stars | **+10** |
| Make Today's Special | **+25** |
| Read a lesson | **+5** |
| Get a quiz answer right | **+15** |

### 8. 📲 Put it on your phone

<img src="docs/screens/13-install.webp" alt="Install guide" width="270" align="right">

Coffee Ghor installs like a real app, with its own icon, full screen and no browser bars. **It works offline too**: once opened, it still loads in a kitchen with no signal.

The app spots your device and shows the right steps:

- **iPhone (Safari):** Share, then *Add to Home Screen*.
- **Android (Chrome):** one tap install when your phone supports it, or the menu steps if not.
- **Inside Instagram, Facebook and similar apps:** it tells you to open it in your real browser first.

You can close the prompt with the **✕** and find it again in Pantry any time.

**Moving to a new phone?** Pantry has **Copy my progress**. Paste the code on the other phone and your level, ratings and settings come with you. Every code is checked before anything in it is used, so a code from someone else can't break your app.

## 🛠 Tech stack

Coffee Ghor is deliberately simple. **One page and one script.** No framework, no build step, no backend, no tracking, and no requests to anyone else's servers.

| Layer | What we use | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **App** | `index.html` (page and styles) and `app.js` (plain JavaScript) | Opens instantly, easy to read, nothing to install or compile |
| **Data** | The browser's `localStorage`, checked on every load | Your progress stays on your phone. No accounts, no server |
| **Offline** | A service worker, network first | Always the newest version when online, still opens with no signal |
| **Security** | Strict Content Security Policy, security headers in `vercel.json`, one gate that cleans all saved and imported data | A pasted progress code can't run code or break the app |
| **Install** | Web app manifest, Apple touch icon, PNG icons | Adds to the Home Screen and opens full screen like a native app |
| **Screen** | Screen Wake Lock API | Keeps the screen on while you're cooking |
| **Design** | OKLCH colour tokens with light and dark mode | Warm coffee tones that stay readable in both modes |
| **Type** | Young Serif for headings, Atkinson Hyperlegible for text, served from this site (SIL Open Font License) | Friendly headings, body text built for easy reading, and no font requests to Google |
| **Animation** | Inline SVG scenes on one shared kitchen counter, CSS keyframes, a timer-driven `--t` variable | 20-plus scenes that follow the timer, plus confetti and reward moments, with no images or libraries. Still, but informative, under reduced motion |
| **Sizing** | iOS type scale (17 px body), 44 px touch targets | Feels at home on an iPhone and is easy to tap |
| **Icons** | Hand drawn inline SVG set | Sharp at any size, no icon library to load |
| **Hosting** | Vercel, linked to this GitHub repo | Every push to `main` goes live in seconds |
| **Testing** | Node.js and Playwright (headless Chromium) | Checks every recipe and every screen automatically |

### How the code is organised

The page and its styles are in `index.html`. The code is in `app.js`, top to bottom:

- **Settings:** moka pot table (`MOKA`), pitcher sizes, grinder types, and the default state.
- **The gate:** `normalize()` rebuilds every saved or imported value from known options and sane numbers.
- **Recipes:** `RECIPES` holds the 14 drinks and their ratio limits. `plan()` does the maths. `buildSteps()` writes the steps in English and Banglish.
- **Ratings and advice:** `ISSUES` and `advice()` turn "too bitter" into a fix that fits your grinder and roast.
- **Scenes:** `S2` and `serveScene()` draw the scene for each step's action (`a:` on every step) from shared parts (`burner()`, `thermo()`, `P.moka()` and friends) placed on the counter with `put()`.
- **Learn:** `CATS` holds the lessons and quizzes. `LEVELS`, `BADGES` and `reward()` run the XP system; `special()` picks Today's Special.
- **Screens:** small `render*` functions draw each view. One click handler routes every tap using `data-*` attributes.

## ✅ How we keep the recipes honest

Every change runs through three automatic checks in a real headless browser.

**Recipe check:** `tests/coffee-eval.js`

Tries **every drink** with every mix of people, strength, roast, sugar, kitchen tools, moka pot size, pitcher size and grinder type. That's **12,096 combinations**, each checked against **31 coffee rules**:

- Café ratios for every drink
- Kettle on before grinding, milk heated right before frothing
- Never overfill the pitcher or the grinder
- Safe moka pot handling, including "never leave it unattended"
- Unsweetened cocoa warnings for mochas
- Italian Moka: cold water, low heat, and a cremina only when there is sugar
- Frappes: coffee chilled before blending, never more than 3 drinks in the blender
- English and Banglish steps say the same numbers
- Every step has its animated scene, the same in both languages

Current score: **100%**.

**Layout check:** `tests/spacing-check.js`

Opens every screen and pop-up at 375 px and 430 px wide, in light and dark mode, and flags any text that touches or spills past the edge of its box. Current result: **0 problems**.

**Security check:** `tests/security-check.js`

Pastes a hostile progress code, opens the app with corrupt saves (missing data, unknown values, 3 million people), folds 400 old drinks, blocks the page mid-timer and presses Back. Checks that no script runs, nothing crashes, XP never drops and the timer stays right. Current result: **20 of 20**.

```bash
npm i -D playwright
node tests/coffee-eval.js index.html
node tests/spacing-check.js index.html
node tests/security-check.js index.html
```
